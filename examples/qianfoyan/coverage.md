# Qianfoyan Source Pack — Evidence Coverage

This document records what the public-source pack can and cannot establish. Missing evidence is not treated as poor performance.

## Public evidence currently supports

- **Resource base:** national-level grotto heritage, irrigation heritage, landscape and adjacent cultural assets are documented by official sources.
- **Peak visitor volume:** the 2025 May Day holiday produced substantial traffic at Dongfengyan-Qianfoyan.
- **Revenue structure:** an official 2025 response discloses strong dependence on admission revenue and a low share from secondary-consumption products.
- **Peak capacity constraint:** the operator reports that core heritage spaces can approach saturation during holidays.
- **Prior investment:** visitor infrastructure and 4A-standard upgrades have already received material investment.
- **Interpretation/digital work:** grotto digitization and a digital exhibition function have been developed or were under development.
- **Institutional linkage:** the county itself identified insufficient linkage mechanisms with the wider Sichuan-Chongqing grotto network and began cooperation with Dazu.
- **Future product direction:** research tourism, cultural products and surrounding place-making are explicit policy/planning directions.
- **Qualitative visitor signal:** a small OTA sample shows both strong heritage appreciation and some value-for-money / dedicated-trip concerns.

## Critical evidence that is still missing

| Evidence need | Why it matters | Suggested method |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly and weekday/weekend visitor distribution | Peak-period traffic may hide weak normal-period demand | ticketing / gate data by day |
| Primary trip purpose | Distinguishes a destination visit from incidental or bundled traffic | visitor intercept survey |
| Visitor origin and segment | Needed before interpreting awareness or product fit | ticketing profile + survey |
| Dwell time by zone | Tests whether heritage depth translates into time spent | anonymized tracking / observation |
| Full visitor-journey observation | Reveals where interpretation or wayfinding actually breaks | field walk + shadowing |
| Interpretation comprehension | A digital hall can exist without producing understanding | exit survey / short recall test |
| Product catalogue and attach rate | Required to distinguish lack of products from weak product conversion | operator sales data |
| Per-capita secondary spend | Ticket dependence alone does not identify why conversion is weak | POS / operator finance data |
| UGC topic distribution | Needed to test what visitors actually remember and retell | broader OTA/social sample |
| Repeat visitation and referral | Required for ADVOCACY rather than simple satisfaction | CRM / survey |
| Local business spillover | Scenic-area revenue is not the same as local economic value | merchant survey + payment/footfall data |
| Off-peak capacity utilization | Peak saturation and annual underuse can coexist | timed traffic data |
| Community/operator role in new content production | Needed before assessing REGENERATION | stakeholder interviews |
| Actual status of planned new place-making projects | Plans must not be counted as operating products | field verification + project status records |

## Evidence discipline

The pack intentionally does **not** conclude that Qianfoyan has a `RESOURCE → MEANING`, `EXPERIENCE → PRODUCT`, or any other Gap yet. Those remain hypotheses to be tested only after Findings are reviewed and missing evidence is made visible.

The most valuable outcome of this pack may be an `UNKNOWN`, not a diagnosis.
