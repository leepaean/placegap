# Diagnostic Data Lifecycle

A PlaceGap diagnosis should remain inspectable as it evolves.

1. Evidence is collected.
2. Findings are proposed.
3. A human accepts, edits, or rejects Findings.
4. Gap candidates are proposed.
5. Hypotheses are created.
6. Supporting and contradicting Evidence is attached.
7. Evidence Needs record what is missing.
8. New Evidence may later weaken or strengthen existing Hypotheses.

Future versions should preserve revisions rather than overwrite the history that explains why a diagnosis changed.
