# Decision Log

## 2026-08-29 — Do not build a generic tourism AI assistant

**Decision:** PlaceGap will focus on evidence-backed diagnosis before strategy.

**Reason:** Generic planning, marketing, and report generation are highly substitutable by general-purpose LLMs and existing tourism platforms.

## 2026-08-29 — Avoid numerical place scores in v0.1

**Decision:** Use qualitative confidence and explicit evidence gaps.

**Reason:** Pseudo-precise scoring would imply measurement validity that the current evidence model cannot justify.

## 2026-08-29 — Keep AI-assisted, human-owned diagnostic state

**Decision:** AI may propose; humans accept, edit, or reject.

**Reason:** Place diagnosis includes normative, cultural, and context-sensitive judgments that should not be silently delegated to a model.
