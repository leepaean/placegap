# Non-goals for v0.1

PlaceGap is deliberately not trying to become a complete tourism platform.

Do not add before validation:

- itinerary or travel recommendation features;
- automatic project planning;
- investment-return modelling;
- destination marketing copy;
- automated social-platform scraping;
- numerical place rankings;
- large management dashboards;
- multi-agent company simulations;
- generalized knowledge-base features unrelated to diagnosis.

A narrow prototype that proves its diagnostic advantage is more valuable than a broad demo that proves nothing.
