# Open Research Questions

PlaceGap is an experimental product and methodology. Important unresolved questions include:

1. When does a structural Gap represent a real bottleneck rather than a convenient narrative?
2. How should source independence be evaluated across copied or derivative sources?
3. How should conflicting visitor, operator, official, and field evidence be weighted without hiding normative choices?
4. Can qualitative confidence remain useful without becoming arbitrary?
5. What parts of place diagnosis can be standardized across heritage sites, historic towns, cultural districts, museums, and rural destinations?
6. Does persistent diagnostic state create enough value beyond a strong expert prompt to justify software?

These questions should be tested rather than answered by product rhetoric.
