# Glossary

**Evidence** — source material or data with provenance.

**Finding** — an atomic statement directly supportable by linked Evidence.

**Gap** — a suspected structural break between diagnostic dimensions.

**Hypothesis** — an interpretation that attempts to explain a Gap or observed condition.

**Contradicting Evidence** — evidence that weakens, complicates, or conflicts with a Hypothesis.

**Alternative Explanation** — a competing interpretation that could explain the same observed Findings.

**Evidence Need** — a question or missing datum required to strengthen, weaken, or resolve a Hypothesis.

**Diagnostic Scope** — an explicit boundary defining what a Place analysis is and is not trying to judge.
